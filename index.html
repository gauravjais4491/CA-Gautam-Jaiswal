About Our Firm

Built on Integrity.
Driven by Results.

Gautam Jaiswal & Associates (GJA) is a dynamic, ICAI-registered Chartered Accountancy firm based in Pune, Maharashtra. Founded in 2025 by CA Gautam Jaiswal, the firm was built with a singular vision — to be a trusted financial partner for businesses at every stage of their journey.

We combine the rigour of traditional CA services — audit, taxation, and compliance — with forward-looking advisory capabilities in Virtual CFO services, startup fundraising, and strategic business growth. Our partners bring complementary expertise, ensuring clients receive holistic, end-to-end financial guidance.

At GJA, we believe that every business deserves access to big-firm expertise with the personal attention of a boutique practice. We are deeply committed to the ethical standards and professional conduct guidelines set by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India.
