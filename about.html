About GJA

Big-Firm Expertise.
Boutique-Firm Attention.

Gautam Jaiswal & Associates (GJA) is a dynamic, ICAI-registered Chartered Accountancy firm founded in Pune, Maharashtra. We combine the precision of traditional CA practice — audit, tax, and compliance — with forward-looking strategic advisory that helps businesses grow with confidence.

“Every business deserves access to big-firm expertise delivered with the personal attention of a boutique practice — with precision, integrity, and genuine care.”

We operate in strict adherence to all guidelines, ethics, and regulations issued by the ICAI — including Standards on Auditing (SAs), Guidance Notes, and the ICAI Code of Ethics. All engagements are conducted with complete discretion.
